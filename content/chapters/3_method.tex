\section{Method}
The submission and review rules for ACCV 2026 are defined in the official ACCV 2026 Author Guidelines, Reviewer Guidelines, and Area Chair Guidelines. By submitting a paper to ACCV 2026, the authors acknowledge that they have read the Author Guidelines and that the submission complies with those rules.

Accepted papers will be published in the Springer LNCS proceedings. Authors are responsible for ensuring that their submissions comply with the applicable formatting, anonymity, originality, copyright, and ethical requirements described in the ACCV 2026 guidelines.
